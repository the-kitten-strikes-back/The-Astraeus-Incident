I got inspired by Doom and created a raycaster:

A psychological sci-fi FPS where you awaken aboard a drifting deep-space vessel with fragmented systems, corrupted logs, and a mystery that defies comprehension. Navigate shifting corridors, uncover impossible truths, and confront an anomaly that distorts more than just space.

What began as a routine expedition has become something far worse.

Corridors shift. Signals repeat. Time fractures.

An impossible anomaly has formed near the ship—a gravitational singularity that doesn't just distort space, but memory, perception… and something deeper. Echoes of the crew linger in broken transmissions. As you navigate the collapsing interior of Astraeus, you must piece together what happened, uncover the truth behind the anomaly, and decide whether escape is even possible.

**Or if you were ever meant to leave at all.**
